# Class Description: AuthActivity

#### 1. General Information
- **Class Name:** `AuthActivity`
- **Type:** Activity
- **Role in Application:** Authentication & Registration screen. Handles mode toggling between Login (Email/Password) and Registration (Email/Password/Confirm/Display Name), Google Sign-In, and security explanation.
- **Interaction:** Uses `AuthManager` to persist login session and redirects to `MainActivity`.

#### 2. Simplified Explanation
**Like a club membership desk:** Shows your membership card (login) or registers a new card with your name and email so you can join the games!
