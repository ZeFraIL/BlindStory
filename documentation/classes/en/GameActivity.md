# Class Description: GameActivity

#### 1. General Information
- **Class Name:** `GameActivity`
- **Type:** Activity
- **Role in Application:** Active game screen showing **only the previous phrase** ("sheet of paper"), 60s turn timer, 180-char limit counter, phrase submission, voting, and turn skipping.
- **Interaction:** Passes story phrases to `FinalStoryActivity` upon completion.

#### 2. Simplified Explanation
**Like the folded paper game:** You read only the last line, write your continuation in 60 seconds, and pass it on!
