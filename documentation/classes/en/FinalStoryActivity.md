# Class Description: FinalStoryActivity

#### 1. General Information
- **Class Name:** `FinalStoryActivity`
- **Type:** Activity
- **Role in Application:** Final story screen displaying full completed story with author credits, emoji reactions, AI commentator analysis, rematch, and story copying.
- **Interaction:** Receives phrases from `GameActivity`.

#### 2. Simplified Explanation
**Like unfolding the paper at the end:** Everyone reads the hilarious full story together, reacts with emojis, and reads the AI comment!
